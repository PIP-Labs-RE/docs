## The DATA Foundation

The DATA Foundation is building the source of truth for AI data. Its core product, **Trace**, is an onchain registry for rights-cleared AI training data: it records where each piece of data came from and the rights it was contributed under.

DATA is becoming a protocol on [Sui](https://sui.io), joining a stack built for AI workloads: Walrus for storage, Seal for access control, and Nautilus for verifiable compute.

<Note>
  Data could be an image, a song, a dataset, an RWA, AI training data, or
  anything in-between.
</Note>

<Accordion title="Why a DATA Foundation?" icon="face-thinking">
Data is the most valuable asset of the AI era, yet the systems we use to share it weren't built for it. Creators lose control and credit the moment their work goes online; AI is trained on copyrighted and unconsented data; and there's no portable, verifiable record of where any of it came from.

Trace gives every piece of data a portable, verifiable record of where it came from and under what terms it was contributed. On Sui, sourcing, storage, access control, and inference can live on one stack, so Trace can prove and verify every step.

</Accordion>

## Quick FAQs

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What's the token?">
  $DATA. It remains the token of the DATA protocol on Sui, carried over
  one-to-one, with holder economics and governance rights at least equivalent
  to today's.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to the DATA Network?">
  The DATA Network will be upgraded to run as a protocol natively on Sui, and
  its independent layer 1 chain will be retired. It keeps running until the
  upgrade is complete. See [DATA on Sui](/data-on-sui) for details, and the
  [Legacy Network](/network/overview) docs for the current chain.
</Accordion>
