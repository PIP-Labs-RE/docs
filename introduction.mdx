## The DATA Foundation

The DATA Foundation is the home for data, built on a purpose-built layer 1 blockchain. It provides verifiable data provenance and programmable confidentiality.

Every piece of data on the DATA Foundation can be:

- **Proven**: a verifiable, portable record of where it came from and under what terms it was contributed
- **Confidential**: encrypted so only the parties you authorize can ever decrypt it

<Note>
  Data could be an image, a song, a dataset, an RWA, AI training data, or
  anything in-between.
</Note>

## How It Fits Together

The DATA Network is a purpose-built layer 1 that combines the best of the EVM and the Cosmos SDK. It is 100% EVM-compatible, with a CometBFT-based consensus layer for fast finality and cheap transactions.

On top of the network, Trace and CDR work together:

- **Trace** records where data came from and the terms it was contributed under.
- **CDR** keeps that data encrypted and gates decryption on-chain.

<Accordion title="Why a DATA Foundation?" icon="face-thinking">
Data is the most valuable asset of the AI era, yet the systems we use to share it weren't built for it. Creators lose control and credit the moment their work goes online; AI is trained on copyrighted and unconsented data; and there's no portable, verifiable record of where any of it came from.

Data registered on the DATA Foundation carries its provenance with it through Trace and can stay confidential through CDR, with access controlled on-chain.

</Accordion>
